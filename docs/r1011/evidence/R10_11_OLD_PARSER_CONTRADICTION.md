English Channel and its refined same-location form in Newfoundland.

root-face extraction, or the low-bit shell reading is NOT the final
semantic truth for these words. Note also the old low-3-bit reading of
`165892733` is 5, while its decimal terminal is 3 — one more marker
divergence for the firewall.
