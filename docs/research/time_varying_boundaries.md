# Time-Varying Boundaries and Impedance Gates

Status: PUBLIC_RESEARCH. NO_PHYSICAL_CLAIM_ADVANCED.

## Purpose

Translate the project phrases "chopped phonons" and "photon boundary
timing" into conventional wave physics: a wavepacket interacting
with a boundary whose parameters change during the interaction.

## External anchors

Time-reflection of microwaves by a fast optically controlled time
boundary (Nature Communications s41467-024-51171-6) and linear
frequency conversion by time-variant metasurfaces (Nature Photonics
s41566-018-0259-4) establish that temporal boundaries translate
wave spectra. The superconducting-circuit dynamical Casimir
experiment (Nature 479 376-379) shows rapid electrical-length
modulation as a boundary-condition analogue.

A parameter change during wave interaction produces partial
reflection, partial transmission, sidebands, and mode mixing.

## Energy and momentum accounting

Boundary switching work supplies or removes energy: the switch or
pump is the energy source and the accounting must show it. Sidebands
or frequency translation are the observables of that exchange. A
momentum ledger is required before any force interpretation is even
considered, and no force acceptance happens until conventional nulls
and that momentum ledger close.
DCE is an analogy, not a power claim: the dynamical Casimir lane
stays a high-frequency conceptual analogue with no bench path and no
energy-harvest framing.

## Bench observables

Ring-down response before and after a boundary switch; sideband
spectrum around the drive; acoustic-to-optical phase modulation with
a laser probe; S-parameter changes across switching states.

## Claim boundary

This lane claims spectral translation and mode mixing under
parameter switching, which is textbook time-varying wave physics.
It does not claim energy gain, propulsion, or any anomalous effect.
