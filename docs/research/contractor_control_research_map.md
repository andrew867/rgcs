# Contractor and DOE/AEC Research Map

Status: PUBLIC_RESEARCH. SOURCE_PROVENANCE_RECORD. NO_PHYSICAL_CLAIM_ADVANCED.

## Purpose

Add a research-targeting map that follows technical capability
through contractors and facilities. The model chain:

```text
mathematical or physical principle
-> patent, paper, or internal report
-> contractor capability
-> government range or lab programme
-> procurement, range support, and public traces
```

The DOE/AEC/contractor lane is a higher-yield research route than
CIA paperwork for this physics and patent programme. That is a
statement about research efficiency, nothing else.

## External anchors

NTIS PB97123947 (Operation Plumbbob timing and firing report, EG&G
responsibility for timing and firing signals), US3207947A (triggered
spark gap) and US2933647A (electric discharge system) for the EG&G
pulsed-power lineage, DOE history for the Manhattan Project to AEC
to DOE chain, and the JT4 public site for current J-Tech II range
support. Entities tracked: AEC, DOE, NTS/NNSS, EG&G, Raytheon
Services Nevada, REECO, JT3/JT4, AECOM, Amentum, LANL, LLNL,
Sandia, Nellis/NTTR, Tonopah Test Range, and the Janet/special
projects transport lane.

## RGCS operator

A provenance graph: entities, capabilities, contracts, periods, and
primary documents, each row carrying a source-quality score and a
claim boundary. Accepted outputs are the research graph, patent
lineage map, contractor capability table, facility and contract
chronology, and source-quality scoring.

## Bench observables

None. This is an archival research lane; its outputs are documents
and graphs, not measurements.

## Claim boundary

Forbidden outputs, refused outright: any claim that this map proves
recovered non-human technology, proves reverse engineering, proves
who controls any facility, or proves any agency irrelevant. The map
is research targeting over public records. Historical contractor
control structure is recorded as history, not as evidence of
anomalous programmes.
