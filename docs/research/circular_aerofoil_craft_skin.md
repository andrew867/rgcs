# Circular aerofoil craft-skin concept (source-language)

Status: source-language concept record for the craft-docs lane only.
It does not enter Phryll cone sizing, bench designs, or any generator
code, and no claim of lift, thrust, or field generation is made — the
project refuses those promotions without measurement.

## The concept, as recorded

- A curved, circular craft skin — a circular aerofoil — is described
  in source material as the outer working surface of the craft.
- The source frames its action through a **pressure-differential
  analogy**: as a wing's curved surface is associated with a pressure
  difference in ordinary aerodynamics, the source language assigns the
  curved circular skin an analogous role for its claimed medium. This
  is an analogy in the source narrative, not a mechanism established
  here.
- The source language further associates the skin with **phonon /
  energy-density** vocabulary. That vocabulary is preserved verbatim
  as source language; it is not adopted as physics by this project.

## Conventional research handles

If any part of the concept is ever tested, the conventional,
measurable implementations to attack first are:

| Handle | Existing RGCS lane |
|---|---|
| surface-wave coupling on a curved conductor/dielectric skin | `rgcs_surface_wave`, SSPP corrugated-waveguide lane |
| acoustic / structural-acoustic skin modes | resonator platform, mechanical excitation lane |
| magnetoelastic coupling in a skin layer | magneto-acoustic / pulsed-coil lane |
| phonon-polariton coupling in skin materials | alpha-quartz polariton / hyperbolic-materials lane |

Each handle is a measurement target, not an endorsement. No skin
prototype exists; nothing here is a build instruction.

## Refused claims

The project does not claim the circular aerofoil skin produces lift,
propulsion, pressure differentials in any exotic medium, or energy
extraction. The pressure-differential analogy and phonon/energy-density
wording are source-language records only.
