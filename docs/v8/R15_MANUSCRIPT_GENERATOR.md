# R15 P31 — Experiment Manuscript Generator

A manuscript is assembled deterministically from phase reports, every section within the software claim ceiling.

- **Module:** `r15/manuscript.py`
- **Tests:** `tests/v8/test_manuscript.py`
- **Claim cap:** nothing measured; `PHYSICAL_VALIDATION_NOT_CLAIMED`.

## Negative results

- Every section carries a claim class; a section claiming a measurement class is refused (ceiling is the software class).
- Results sections state their numbers are synthetic; nothing measured.
