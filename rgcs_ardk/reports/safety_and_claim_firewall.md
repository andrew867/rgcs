# Safety and Claim Firewall

RGCS-ARDK-001 is a stationary, current-limited measurement rig for testing
controllable annular electromagnetic field asymmetry. It is not a craft,
flight article, energy source, or mechanical-performance test.

The sole primary observable is `DeltaB`. Software contains no executable
force/thrust namespace and no wall-power performance mapping. Missing ordinary
explanations remain control-work items, never source attribution or new-physics
evidence.

Required safeguards are default-off operation, hardware current limiting,
fusing, thermal sensors and derating, overtemperature abort, enclosure abort,
heartbeat abort, sensor-failure abort, shielded wiring, strain relief, and
low-power dummy-load commissioning. Magnetic-field access controls apply.
